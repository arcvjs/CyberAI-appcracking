# Asset provenance

`screenshots/` contains the ten screenshots supplied by the project owner for the 12-slide exhibition deck: Prism, Folio, Afterlight, their demo code, and IDA's patch view. The screenshots were copied from temporary attachments so rebuilding no longer depends on the original machine's Temp folder.

The CIPHER festival poster visible in Prism and the presentation was supplied by the user. Its event and university marks belong to their respective owners. No blanket open-source license is asserted for third-party artwork. Prism's earlier mountain image has its source noted in `Prism/Desktop/Assets/mountain-source.txt`.

The app source remains separate from these presentation assets. Screenshots document a teaching demo and do not imply affiliation with commercial products referenced by the project.
